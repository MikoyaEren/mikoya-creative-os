# Mikoya Matcha — "Hero vs. Villain" AI ad storyboard

**Script:** *AI – Hero Villain – Timestamps* (German, 1:40)
**Reference:** Actora "Fix your Thyroid, Naturally", Gethookd ad 117782657 (3:18, 9:16, rated "Winning", running 117 days). All 198 s were inspected: 113 detected cuts, a mid-shot frame of every shot, and the full timed transcript.

## Assumptions

- **"Guter Matcha kommt rein" (00:38–00:40)** is treated as a stage direction with no voiceover (Clip 17). If it should be spoken, give it to the narrator voice.
- **Voices** come from the calibration voice locks adapted to German, because the audio couldn't be listened to directly. The reference uses a villain voice that's separate from the warm female product voice, and this storyboard keeps that split.
- **Language:** dialogue stays in German, word for word. Prompts are in English for the image and video models.
- **Captions**, plus the "40€" and "30 Tage" text, are added in the edit, not generated.
- **Visual concept:** the owner's Hero-vs-Villain concept board (`brand-references/mikoya/style/hero-villain-concept.webp`) is the primary style reference. It defines a cinematic 3D look, the realistic woman, the black Mikoya pouch with vivid green powder, the dirty beige "BÖSER MATCHA" pouch and the stomach scenes. It is a concept, not the final character sheets: those stay *pending* until the owner uploads them.
- **Stomach scenes:** following the concept's "Szenenbeispiele im Magen", four clips now play inside the stomach:
  - Clip 01: the villain greets from the stomach as the murky matcha splashes in.
  - Clip 17: good matcha arrives.
  - Clip 20: the confrontation.
  - Clip 31: good matcha wins and the villain is blasted away.

  All other locations, actions, camera moves and dialogue are unchanged.
- **Packaging:** the current pouch (product photos in `brand-references/mikoya/product/`) is the single source of truth. The label reads "MIKOYA" / "MATCHA" / "100% Ceremonial", with the green leaf and "30g". The preparation-set photo still shows the retired "JPN MATCHA" pouch, so in Clips 37–38 the pouch references are listed first and decide the label.
- **Reference images:** every clip lists the files to attach, taken from `brand-references/mikoya/manifest.json`. The pouch and set photos are already available. The character sheets and style frame are marked *pending* until you upload them.
- **Claims:** the heavy-metal/lead line is shown only as an illustration (magnifying glass, cartoon x-ray), with no study documents or charts on screen. Check you can back it up before it runs in DE.
- **Pacing:** the reference cuts every 1.75 s on average. This storyboard averages 2.4 s per clip so each one can be generated on its own. Trim heads and tails in the edit to get closer to the reference rhythm.

## Reference-ad blueprint

- **Format:** 720×1280 (9:16), polished AI-made Pixar-like 3D cartoon with warm cinematic light. *This storyboard follows the owner's concept board instead: a cinematic, more realistic 3D look and a realistic woman.* Purple crackle means bad; gold and green glows mean good.
- **Hook:** the villain introduces itself in first person, sitting on the organ it damages and glaring at camera. Each phrase gets a literal image in the next cut (a calendar for "years", a sleeping woman for "quietly").
- **Pacing:** 113 cuts in 198 s, about 1.75 s per shot. The mechanism section runs 1–2 s per cut and the transformation timeline 2–3 s. Cuts come several times per sentence, often per phrase.
- **Camera:** one centred character in medium close-up, alternating with macro "inside the body" shots and medium lifestyle shots. Slow push-ins, energy bursts and beams, hard cuts with no transitions.
- **Visual devices:** every player is personified, including the villain, each failed alternative, each ingredient, and the "bad hormone" as angry black rocks. Each has one power, shown as a single action or beam. When the protagonist improves, she gets a green aura.
- **Roles:**
  - The villain owns 0–35 s.
  - The failed alternatives explain why they don't work.
  - The product is a talking bottle with eyes and mouth on the label, thin black limbs, white cartoon gloves and white sneakers. It gets about 8 hero shots on the same sunny kitchen counter.
  - The recurring woman (same face and outfit) is silent. The husband and doctor are one-offs.
- **Captions:** one word at a time, bold white comic-style uppercase with a thick black outline, in the lower-middle of the frame.
- **Close:** 60-day guarantee, bundle (glowing bottles side by side), "small company" factory shot, product held to camera, phone with a big green checkmark.
